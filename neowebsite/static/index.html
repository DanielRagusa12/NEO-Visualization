How to read these comparisons

Models and rotation periods are illustrative, not measured reconstructions. Tiny items are enlarged for visibility. Everest comparisons use summit elevation above sea level, not local mountain relief.

One lunar distance is the average distance between Earth and the Moon. A potentially hazardous classification describes an object's size and orbit, not a prediction of an impact.
